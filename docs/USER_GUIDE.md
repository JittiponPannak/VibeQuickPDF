# User Guide

This guide walks through every feature in **VibeQuickPDF** (`v1.3.2`).

---

## 1. Home Screen

The Home Screen displays all PDF and ZIP files registered in VibeQuickPDF, sorted newest first.

- **Open a file**: Tap any card to open the PDF or ZIP with your device's default viewer.
- **Share a file**: Tap the blue **Share** icon (`Icons.share`) to open the system share sheet. If the share action is cancelled or fails to complete, a retry prompt is shown.
- **Delete / Remove a file**: Tap the red **Delete** icon (`Icons.delete`):
  - **App-created files**: Prompts to confirm permanent deletion from storage and removal from the list.
  - **Imported PDFs** (marked with an **Imported** / **นำเข้า** badge): Gives you two choices:
    - **Remove from List**: Removes the entry from VibeQuickPDF only; the original file stays untouched on your device.
    - **Delete File**: Deletes the underlying file from device storage (if writable) and removes it from the list.
- **Create a new file**: Tap the **Create PDF** button at the bottom of the screen.

### Overflow Menu (`⋮`)

Tap the top-right overflow menu on the Home Screen for:
1. **Refresh** — Re-checks registered files, updates sizes/names, and prunes entries whose files were deleted externally.
2. **Import PDF From File** *(Android only)* — Opens the Android document picker to add an existing PDF to your VibeQuickPDF list. Duplicate imports (including PDFs already generated by VibeQuickPDF) are automatically detected and prevented.
3. **Switch to Dark Mode / Light Mode** — Toggles between Light and Dark theme for the current session.
4. **Language** — Switch at runtime between:
   - **System Default** (defaults to Thai if the system language is not Thai/English)
   - **ภาษาไทย (Thai)**
   - **English**

> **Note:** Theme and language selections apply immediately for the current session and reset to system defaults when the app is restarted.

## 2. Creating a PDF or ZIP

Tap **Create PDF** on the Home Screen to open the Conversion Screen.

### Adding Images
- **Camera (`Take Photo`)**: Capture a new photo directly with your device's camera.
- **Gallery (`Gallery`)**: Select multiple existing photos at once.
- Once at least one image is selected, you can continue adding images via the **Add Photo** tile at the end of the grid, the header **Add Photo** button, or the camera/gallery icons in the top AppBar.

### Reordering & Removing Pages
Each image card displays its current page number (`1`, `2`, `3`, ...):
- **Drag and Drop**: Press and hold any image (~150 ms) and drag it onto another tile (or onto the **Add Photo** tile to move it to the end).
- **Tap-to-Reorder Modal**: Tap any image to open a bottom sheet with quick buttons:
  - **Move to First**
  - **Move Previous**
  - **Move Next**
  - **Move to Last**
  - **Delete** (remove that page)
- **Quick Delete**: Tap the `×` badge in the top-right corner of any image tile.
- **Clear All**: Tap **Clear** or the trash-sweep icon in the AppBar (asks for confirmation).

### Export Settings
Inside the settings card at the top of the Conversion Screen:
1. **File Name**: Enter a custom base name. If left blank, defaults to `เอกสาร` (Thai) or `Document` (English).
2. **Export Format**: Choose between **PDF** and **ZIP**.
3. **Merge Toggle**:
   - **ON (default)**: Combines all selected images into a single `.pdf` (1 page per image) or a single `.zip` (images named `<name>_1.ext`, `<name>_2.ext`, zero-padded for 10+ / 100+ files).
   - **OFF (Split)**: Creates one separate `.pdf` or `.zip` per image, named `<name>_1`, `<name>_2`, etc. (no suffix if only 1 image is selected).

### Previewing a PDF
Tap **Preview** (above the Save/Share buttons or the eye icon in the AppBar):
1. VibeQuickPDF generates a temporary merged PDF in the cache directory and opens it in your device's PDF viewer.
2. Returning to VibeQuickPDF shows a **PDF Preview** bottom sheet with **Re-open** and **Close Preview** buttons.
3. Dismissing the sheet (or leaving the Conversion Screen) automatically deletes the temporary preview file.

### Saving vs. Sharing
- **Save to Device**:
  - On **Android**, saves into public `Documents/VibeQuickPDF/` (avoiding filename collisions with `_1`, `_2`, ... suffixes), registers the file in the Home list, and returns to the Home Screen.
  - On **non-Android** platforms, saves into `<AppDocuments>/VibeQuickPDF/` and registers the file.
- **Share**:
  - Generates temporary file(s) and opens the OS share sheet directly without adding them to `Documents/VibeQuickPDF/` or the Home list.
  - Includes a share caption (`Created with VibeQuickPDF` / `สร้างด้วย VibeQuickPDF`).
  - If sharing is incomplete, offers a **Retry** dialog.

## 3. Sharing Files *Into* VibeQuickPDF (Android)

You can share files directly from your Gallery, File Manager, Messaging apps, or Browser to VibeQuickPDF:

- **Supported Images**: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.bmp`, `.gif`, `.heic`, `.heif`, `.tif`, `.tiff` (single or multiple).
- **Supported Archives**: `.zip`, `.tar`, `.tar.gz`, `.tgz`, `.gz` (VibeQuickPDF extracts all image files inside, ignores macOS metadata like `__MACOSX` and hidden `.` files, and sorts them alphabetically by filename).

When VibeQuickPDF receives shared files (whether closed or already open), a bottom sheet presents three choices:
1. **Append to current list**: Adds the incoming images to the end of your current draft list.
2. **Create new list (insert as first element)**: Clears the current draft and starts fresh with the incoming images.
3. **Quick PDF & Share**: Immediately merges the incoming images into a PDF named `QuickPDF` and opens the system share sheet without entering the editor—ideal for quickly converting a received photo or archive and forwarding it as a PDF.
